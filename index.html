Activities and Projects

I have developed expertise in designing fault detection and mitigation systems for RISC-V architectures, focusing on advanced algorithms like LSTM-Autoencoders and Proximal Policy Optimization, along with utilizing microarchitectural simulators such as gem5.

I have developed expertise in utilizing the CGRA SiLago framework to integrate Deep Reinforcement Learning algorithms, as well as employing the NoCDAS framework to implement Transformer models and the computational NoC paradigm.

I have gained experience in selecting and optimizing domain-specific accelerators (DSAs), such as SIMBA, for efficient deep neural network (DNN) inference in wireless IoT devices, ensuring resource-efficient implementations that meet strict latency and performance requirements in industrial applications.

I have developed expertise in indoor localization (the focus of my master’s thesis) through the application of Machine Learning algorithms, Neural Networks, and Particle Filters.

Through the various projects undertaken during my studies, I have acquired a solid understanding of both the theoretical and practical aspects of numerous programming languages.
